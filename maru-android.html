MARU Media Player for Android — official Google Play app

MARU Media Player is a video and audio player for Android phones, tablets and foldable devices. It supports local media playback, subtitle display and Picture-in-Picture (PiP) video playback. Availability of AI subtitle features depends on the plan and in-app terms.
